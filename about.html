Our Story

Built On Curation, Not Mass Production

"We started Sadiya Fashions because Seoul and Shanghai's street style rarely made it to our shelves — so we built the shelf ourselves."

What began as a small edit of Korean and Chinese inspired essentials has grown into a full multi-brand destination for men who want their wardrobe to say something. We work with independent labels who share our obsession with cut, fabric and finishing — never chasing trends for their own sake.

Our Style

Oversized. Editorial. Unbothered.

Dropped shoulders, boxy tees, wide-leg trousers and clean minimal palettes — our style direction takes cues from Seoul's Seongsu-dong studios and Shanghai's gallery-district street style, filtered for the everyday.

Black, white, cream, charcoal and beige form our core palette, finished with the occasional flash of muted gold — because true style doesn't need to shout.
